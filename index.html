About the Team

GeoForce is focused on research, prototyping, and collaborative engineering in robotics, artificial intelligence, and interactive systems. Our mission is to explore innovative ideas, experiment with practical solutions, and develop technology that inspires learning and real impact.

In the RoboCup Japan Open 2025 @Home League, the team worked on a Bridge competition system inspired by the 2024 World Championship @Home Playground rules, emphasizing perception, navigation, object handling, and natural interaction in domestic settings.

Achievements & Highlights

  • Participated in RoboCup Japan Open 2025, held from May 2–5, 2025 at the Shiga Daihatsu Arena in Otsu City, Shiga, Japan.
  • Competed in the @Home Bridge segment under the RoboCup Japan Open league system, with rules based on the 2024 World Championship @Home Playground structure.
  • Built and refined robot capabilities in perception, navigation, task reasoning, and human-centered interaction for domestic service scenarios.
  • Continued exploration of real-time perception, autonomous planning, and responsive system behavior through competition-driven development.
